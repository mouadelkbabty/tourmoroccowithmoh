Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour with Moh? ▾

Message Moh directly on WhatsApp, call, or send an inquiry through the contact form. There are no call centers or booking agents involved — you deal directly with your guide.

Are the tours private? ▾

Yes. Every tour is private and built around you, whether you're traveling solo, as a couple, with family, or in a group.

What languages does Moh speak? ▾

English, French, Spanish, and Darija (Moroccan Arabic).

Can multiple destinations be combined into one itinerary? ▾

Yes, multi-city itineraries are common — for example Marrakech combined with the Sahara, or Fes combined with Chefchaouen and Tangier in the north.

Does Tour Morocco with Moh also plan weddings and celebrations? ▾

Yes. Alongside destination tours, Moh and his wife Lee plan weddings, henna nights, birthdays, honeymoons, and family celebrations across Morocco. See the Celebrations section for more.

How quickly does Moh respond to inquiries? ▾

Usually within a few hours, and often much sooner over WhatsApp.
