About Us

Welcome to our site, Tour Morocco with Moh. I was born and raised in Marrakech, and have been a licensed tour guide for many years now. I speak English, French, Spanish, and of course, Darija, the language of Moroccans.

I formed Tour Morocco with Moh with my wife, Lee, who is a graphic designer and artist. When I’m not guiding tours, or sourcing beautiful Moroccan products, we enjoy touring all over Morocco, and in other parts of the world. I love what I do, and I love meeting new people and showing them my beautiful country.
