A different side of Morocco

Casablanca, Beyond the Airport Layover

Most visitors only see Casablanca's airport on the way to somewhere else — but Morocco's largest city has its own story: a striking Art Deco downtown, a mosque built directly over the Atlantic, and a coastline that locals actually use. It's an easy add-on to any Morocco itinerary, especially if you're flying in or out of Casablanca anyway.

I'll build a half-day or full-day private tour around your flight times or the rest of your trip, so you leave with more than a layover.

What We'll See Together

Hassan II Mosque

One of the largest mosques in the world, with a minaret rising over the ocean itself.

The Corniche & Ain Diab

Casablanca's seafront promenade, cafés, and beach clubs facing the Atlantic.

The Old Medina

A smaller, quieter medina than Marrakech or Fes, with an authentic local pace.

Mohammed V Square

The city's grand Art Deco civic heart, ringed by French-colonial-era architecture.

Habous Quarter

A "new medina" built in the 1930s — traditional Moroccan design with a planned layout.

Layover-Friendly Routing

Tours timed around your flight schedule, ideal for a stopover between international legs.
