About Us

Welcome to Our Morocco

Welcome to our site, Tour Morocco with Moh. I was born and raised in Marrakech, and have been a licensed tour guide for many years now. I speak English, French, Spanish, and of course, Darija, the language of Moroccans.

I formed Tour Morocco with Moh with my wife, Lee, who is a graphic designer and artist. When I'm not guiding tours, or sourcing beautiful Moroccan products, we enjoy touring all over Morocco, and in other parts of the world.

I love what I do, and I love meeting new people and showing them my beautiful country.

Let me know if you're planning a special event. I can organize and plan your birthday party, wedding, or any other special event you may want to have while you're in Morocco. You will enjoy the occasion even more by letting me take care of the details.
