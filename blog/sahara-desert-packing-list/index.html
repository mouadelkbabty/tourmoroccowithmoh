What to Pack for the Sahara

  1. Home
  2. /
  3. Blog
  4. /
  5. Sahara Desert Packing List

The single biggest thing to understand about a Merzouga / Erg Chebbi desert trip is the temperature swing: it can be 30°C+ (86°F+) in the afternoon sun and drop close to freezing once the sun is down, especially outside the summer months. Pack for both, and you'll be fine.

Clothing

  • Layers — a light long-sleeve shirt and loose trousers for sun protection during the day, plus a warm fleece or jacket for the evening and night.
  • A scarf or light shesh/turban — genuinely useful, not just for photos: it protects your face and neck from sun and blowing sand, especially on the camel trek.
  • Comfortable closed shoes for walking on the dunes (sand gets everywhere, but closed shoes keep the worst of it out), plus something easy to slip on and off at camp.
  • A hat with a brim, and sunglasses — desert sun and light-colored sand reflect a lot of glare.

Essentials

  • Sunscreen — high SPF, reapplied more than you'd think you need.
  • A headlamp or small flashlight — desert camps generally run on generators or solar power with limited lighting after dark.
  • A portable phone charger / power bank — outlets at camp are limited or nonexistent.
  • Wet wipes and hand sanitizer — water for washing up is limited at camp.
  • A basic first aid kit — plasters, any personal medication, and rehydration salts if you're prone to dehydration.
  • Small cash (dirhams) — for tips to camel handlers and camp staff.

Nice to Have

  • A camera or phone with a wide-angle lens — the sunrise and sunset over the dunes are the whole point.
  • A light daypack for the camel trek, so your main luggage can stay at the vehicle.
  • Earplugs, if you're a light sleeper — desert camps are usually very quiet, but it's a shared environment.

What You Can Leave Behind

Leave the heavy suitcase in Marrakech or Fes if you can — most desert routes involve a long drive with stops, and a smaller overnight bag makes the whole trip easier. Formal clothing, hair dryers, and anything valuable you're not comfortable getting a little sandy are all safe to skip.
