Your Professional Tour Guide

Welcome to Tour Morocco with Moh. Born and raised in the heart of Marrakech, I am a fully licensed, professional tour guide dedicated to sharing the true spirit of my homeland. I speak English, French, Spanish, and Darija, ensuring a seamless and enriching experience for travelers from all over the world.

Whether you want to explore ancient medinas, journey through the Sahara dunes, or discover hidden artisanal souks, I provide deeply personalized, private itineraries. When I am not guiding, my wife Lee—an artist and graphic designer—and I are constantly exploring to find the most beautiful, off-the-beaten-path locations for your next adventure.
