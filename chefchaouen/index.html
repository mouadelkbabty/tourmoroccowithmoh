Wander the Blue Alleys of Chefchaouen

Tucked into the Rif Mountains, Chefchaouen's medina is painted almost entirely in shades of blue — every wall, staircase, and doorway. It's one of the calmest, most walkable towns in Morocco, and one of the most rewarding for anyone who just wants to wander with a camera.

I know the alleys well enough to keep you away from the crowds, and to make sure you don't miss the viewpoints that make Chefchaouen worth the mountain drive.

What We'll See Together

The Blue Medina

Endless blue-washed alleyways, doorways draped in flowers, and hidden courtyards.

Outa El Hammam Square

The medina's main square, ringed by the old Kasbah and shaded cafés.

Ras El Maa Waterfall

A small waterfall right at the edge of the medina, where locals still wash wool and rugs.

Spanish Mosque Viewpoint

A short hike above town for the best panoramic view over the blue rooftops.

The Kasbah Museum

A 15th-century fortress with gardens and a small ethnographic museum.

Local Weaving & Wool

Handwoven blankets and wool goods, a Rif Mountain craft tradition.
