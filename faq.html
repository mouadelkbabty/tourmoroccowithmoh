Frequently Asked Questions

Everything travelers usually ask before booking a tour with Moh.

How do I book a private tour with Moh? +

Message Moh directly on WhatsApp, by phone, by email, or through the contact form on this site with your travel dates, group size, and what you'd like to see. Moh will reply with a tailored itinerary and pricing for your trip.

What languages does Moh speak? +

Moh speaks English, French, Spanish, and Darija (Moroccan Arabic), so your tour can be guided comfortably in whichever language you prefer.

How much does a tour cost? +

Pricing depends on the destinations, tour length, group size, and any special requests (like a celebration or private transport), so every quote is tailored. Message Moh with your plans for an exact price.

Are tours private, or will we be grouped with strangers? +

All tours with Tour Morocco with Moh are 100% private — it's just you, your group, and Moh (plus a driver on longer trips). There are no shared group tours.

What's the best time of year to visit Morocco? +

Spring (March–May) and fall (September–November) generally offer the most comfortable weather across Morocco's cities and the Sahara. See our seasonal guide for a month-by-month breakdown.

Can Moh help plan a wedding, birthday, or other celebration in Morocco? +

Yes — Moh and his wife Lee regularly plan destination weddings, henna ceremonies, birthdays, and other celebrations in Morocco, handling venues, decor, and logistics alongside the tour itinerary.

Is airport pickup included? +

Airport pickup and drop-off can be arranged as part of your tour — just let Moh know your flight details when booking so transport is ready when you land.

Do I need to tip my guide or driver? +

Tipping isn't required but is appreciated and customary in Morocco for guides and drivers if you're happy with your experience. There's no fixed amount — it's entirely at your discretion.

How safe is traveling in Morocco with a private guide? +

Morocco is a popular and generally safe destination for travelers, and having a licensed local guide like Moh makes navigating cities, medinas, and desert trips considerably easier and safer, since he knows the areas, customs, and language.

What should I pack, and how should I dress? +

Comfortable walking shoes and modest, breathable clothing are recommended for city and medina visits, with warmer layers for desert nights. See our packing list and etiquette guide on the blog for full detail.

What's your cancellation policy? +

Because every tour is private and personalized, cancellation terms are confirmed with you directly at booking. See our Terms & Privacy page for the general policy, or message Moh if your plans change.
