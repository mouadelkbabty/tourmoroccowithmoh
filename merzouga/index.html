The trip most travelers remember most

Into the Sahara Desert

The Erg Chebbi dunes near Merzouga are Morocco's most accessible slice of true Sahara — towering golden dunes that shift color from dawn to dusk. Most visitors reach it as part of a multi-day route from Marrakech or Fes, crossing the High Atlas and the desert gorges on the way.

I'll organize the full route, the camel trek out to camp, and a night under a sky with more stars than you've likely ever seen — whether you're joining as a couple, a family, or a small group.

What the Journey Includes

Erg Chebbi Dunes

Some of the tallest dunes in Morocco, glowing gold at sunrise and sunset.

Camel Trekking

A guided camel caravan out to camp as the sun goes down over the dunes.

Desert Camp Under the Stars

A night in the dunes, far enough from any town for genuinely dark skies.

Dades & Todra Gorges

Dramatic canyon roads on the classic overland route to Merzouga.

Khamlia Gnawa Village

A small village near Merzouga known for its Gnawa music tradition.

Sunrise Over the Dunes

An early climb for the sunrise view most people come to the Sahara for.
