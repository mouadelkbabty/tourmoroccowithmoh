Your guide in Marrakech

Explore Marrakech Like a Local

Marrakech is where most Morocco journeys begin — a maze of a medina, centuries-old palaces, and a main square that never quite goes quiet. As a Marrakech-born, licensed guide, I'll take you off the tourist track and into the city the way I know it: the quiet courtyard riads, the family-run workshops in the souks, and the rooftop views nobody puts on a postcard.

Every tour is private and built around you — whether that's a half-day walk through the medina or a full multi-day Marrakech itinerary combined with a Sahara extension or a celebration.

What We'll See Together

Koutoubia Mosque

Marrakech's tallest and most iconic landmark, and the minaret that anchors the whole city skyline.

Jemaa el-Fnaa Square

The living heart of the medina — storytellers, musicians, and food stalls, by day and by night.

Bahia & El Badi Palaces

Two very different sides of Moroccan royal history — ornate zellige interiors and grand ruined courtyards.

Majorelle Garden

A cobalt-blue garden oasis built by Jacques Majorelle and later owned by Yves Saint Laurent.

The Souks

Spices, lanterns, leather, and carpets — with a local guide to help you shop and bargain fairly.

Gueliz District

Marrakech's modern quarter, for a change of pace between the medina's ancient alleyways.
